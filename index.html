Formula reference

Gas laws

PV = nRT. Use kelvin and an R with pressure–volume units that match. Boyle’s law: P1 V1 = P2 V2 at fixed temperature.

Acids, bases & equilibrium

pH = -log[H⁺] for ideal dilute solutions, and K = products/reactants with coefficients as exponents. Activities matter outside dilute approximations.

Calorimetry

q = mcΔT. Heat gained by the surroundings has the opposite sign to the reaction heat in an insulated system.

Rate laws

rate = k[A]^m[B]^n. Exponents are determined experimentally, not normally from stoichiometric coefficients.

Conversions

n = m / M (mass to moles); M = n / V (molarity). Convert milliliters to liters before computing M.
